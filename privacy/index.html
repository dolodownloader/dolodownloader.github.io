Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the DOLO Android app ("DOLO", "we") collects and how it is used.

Videos you download

Videos are downloaded directly on your device. We do not upload, store or view the links you paste or the files you download.

Account

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We use them to identify your account, your membership status and your invite (referral) code.

Data we store

Account details, the answer to the "How did you hear about us?" question, invite/referral records, membership and payment status (we never receive your full card details), your language setting and an anonymous device identifier used to prevent abuse of referrals and promotions.

Advertising

The free version may show ads from Google AdMob or other ad networks. These partners may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your Android settings. Premium members see no ads.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database and server functions), Google Play Billing and/or a payment provider to process payments, and GitHub to host this website.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your personal data, except an anonymous record kept to prevent abuse of referrals and promotions. You can also contact us via the address below.

Children

DOLO is not intended for children under 13.
